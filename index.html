About

Technical depth, with the leadership to ship

Software developer and entrepreneur with 10+ years building mobile apps, games and interactive experiences. What sets me apart is that I've also run a company: as co-founder and technical director I carried the technical, financial and client side of a studio. That means I work independently, prioritize ruthlessly and manage deadlines without needing to be managed.

I pair deep technical expertise with the leadership to deliver, setting technical direction, leading cross-functional development teams, and owning projects from concept to release. Today I work freelance, building and shipping mobile apps start to finish, including guiding clients through the App Store and Play Store review process.

I learned my craft before AI could write code for me. That foundation, plus years of guiding junior developers, makes me especially effective at using AI now: I know what to delegate, what to question, and how to turn fast suggestions into reliable software.
