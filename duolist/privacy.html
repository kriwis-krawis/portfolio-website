Lists, photos, and scans

DuoList reads text from photos you take or select using Apple’s on-device text recognition. DuoList stores saved lists, scan text, and any retained scan photos in its app data. DuoList does not upload this content to a developer server. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

You can delete saved lists in the app and adjust how many scan photos DuoList keeps per list in Settings. Removing a stored scan photo does not delete the original from Photos. Apple’s device backups may retain copies according to your settings.

Apple Reminders and purchases

If you grant Reminders access, DuoList reads or writes reminders to carry out the actions you choose. If iCloud is enabled for Reminders, Apple can sync those reminders across your devices. You can revoke access in iOS Settings.

Apple processes in-app purchases. DuoList uses StoreKit to check whether the upgrade is unlocked and does not receive your payment card details. See Apple’s Privacy Policy for how Apple handles its services.
